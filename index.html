Marks file format guidance

The uploaded Excel file should contain exactly three columns: BITS ID, Course, and Total Marks.

Total Marks must be whole numbers from 0 to 100. Round fractional marks according to your course policy before uploading — the console never changes marks, and flags fractional values so no student is graded on an unrounded mark.

Students who did not appear for the trimester-end examination and are to be awarded an NC grade should not be included in the file.

This console is intended for grading only those students who appeared for the examination.

Files are processed in your browser. No student data is uploaded anywhere.

    How this console works

    1 · Prepare the marks file

    • Three columns: BITS ID, Course, Total Marks (0–100, whole numbers). Use the Template button for a ready-made file.
    • Leave out students who receive an NC grade (did not appear for the exam).
    • A file may hold several courses; each course is graded separately with its own scheme.

    2 · Upload & validate

    • The console checks for missing columns, empty IDs, duplicate IDs within a course, missing or invalid marks, and marks outside 0–100.
    • Marks are never changed. Rows with errors are left out of analytics, and a course can't be finalized until its data errors are fixed in the source file and it is re-uploaded.

    3 · Configure grades

    • A student gets a grade when their mark is between that grade's Min and Max (both inclusive). Ranges must cover 0–100 with no gaps or overlaps.
    • Auto-link keeps neighbours continuous: changing B's Min moves B-'s Max to one below it.
    • Every edit is a simulation. The simulator shows who would change grade; nothing becomes official until you press Apply changes.
    • Boundary handles on the scale support the keyboard: ←/→ move by 1, PgUp/PgDn by 5.

    4 · Review & export

    • Tick the review box, then Finalize & Download. CSV holds BITS ID, Course, Total Marks and Grade; the Excel report adds Statistics, Grade Distribution and Validation Report sheets.
    • Percentile rank = share of students scoring below the student, counting ties as half.
    • The grading timer starts when you first select a course and pauses when you finalize.
